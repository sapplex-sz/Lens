Lens 技术支持

Lens 是一款 AI 图像案例与提示词灵感工具，支持浏览、搜索、收藏、复制、编辑提示词，并将已加载内容保存在设备上供离线使用。

需要帮助或反馈问题？

请前往 Lens 支持与反馈，说明你的设备型号、iOS 版本、App 版本以及复现步骤。请勿提交私人或敏感信息。

常见问题

首次打开没有内容：首次加载需要网络连接。请检查网络后重新打开 App。

离线时看不到某张图片：只有已经成功加载过的数据和图片会保存在本地；尚未浏览的内容仍需要联网获取。

如何清除本地数据：删除 App 会清除收藏、个人提示词和本地缓存。重新安装后无法恢复这些内容。

内容来源：案例来自公开的开源项目和社区来源。许可与署名说明可在 App 的“关于”页面查看。

Lens Support

Lens is an AI image case and prompt inspiration tool for browsing, searching, saving, copying, and editing prompts. Previously loaded content is stored on the device for offline use.

Need help or want to report an issue?

Visit Lens Support & Feedback and include your device model, iOS version, app version, and steps to reproduce. Do not post private or sensitive information.

Frequently asked questions

No content on first launch: The first load requires an internet connection. Check your network and reopen the app.

An image is unavailable offline: Only data and images that loaded successfully are retained locally. Content you have not viewed still requires a connection.

Removing local data: Deleting the app removes favorites, personal prompts, and cached content. This data cannot be restored after reinstalling.

Content sources: Cases come from public open-source and community sources. Licensing and attribution are available in the app's About page.
