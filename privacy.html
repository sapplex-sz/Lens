Lens 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 25 日

Lens 由 sapplex-sz 提供。本政策说明 Lens 如何处理信息。

我们不收集的数据

Lens 不要求注册账户，不集成广告、分析统计或用户追踪服务，也不会向开发者服务器上传你的搜索、收藏、提示词、个人提示词库或使用记录。

设备上的数据

收藏、个人提示词、版本历史，以及已经浏览过的案例数据和图片缓存，仅保存在你的设备上，用于离线访问和改善加载速度。这些数据不会与开发者同步。你可以通过删除 App 来清除这些本地数据；iOS 也可能根据系统存储策略清理缓存。

联网与第三方内容服务

Lens 会从 jsDelivr 内容分发网络读取公开的案例、提示词和图片。联网请求可能使该服务的运营方按其正常服务流程接收到 IP 地址、请求时间、设备网络信息等技术数据。Lens 不会使用这些信息识别你，也不向该服务发送你的收藏或个人提示词。

外部链接

当你主动打开来源、开源许可或项目链接时，页面会在系统浏览器中打开。第三方网站的数据处理方式受其各自隐私政策约束。

儿童隐私

Lens 不面向儿童设计，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

政策更新与联系我们

若功能或数据处理方式发生实质变化，我们会更新本页面及生效日期。有关隐私的问题，可通过 Lens 支持渠道联系我们。

Lens Privacy Policy

Effective: August 25, 2026

Lens is provided by sapplex-sz. This policy explains how Lens handles information.

Data we do not collect

Lens requires no account and includes no advertising, analytics, or tracking services. Your searches, favorites, prompts, personal prompt library, and usage history are not uploaded to the developer.

Data stored on your device

Favorites, personal prompts, version history, and cached case data and images remain on your device for offline access and faster loading. They are not synchronized with the developer. You can remove this local data by deleting the app; iOS may also remove cached files under its storage policies.

Network access and third-party content delivery

Lens retrieves public cases, prompts, and images from the jsDelivr content delivery network. As part of normal network delivery, its operator may receive technical information such as IP address, request time, and device network information. Lens does not use this information to identify you and does not send your favorites or personal prompts to that service.

External links

Source, open-source license, and project links that you choose to open are displayed in the system browser. Third-party sites process data under their own privacy policies.

Children

Lens is not designed for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date if the app's features or data practices materially change. For privacy questions, contact us through the Lens support channel.
